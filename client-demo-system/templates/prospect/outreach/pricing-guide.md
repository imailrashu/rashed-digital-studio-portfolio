## Recommendation Logic

Choose the smallest package that credibly supports the prospect's confirmed business requirements.

- Consider **Website Essentials** for a focused online presence and straightforward contact journey without advanced guidance, analytics, or integration needs.
- Consider **Business Growth** when a chatbot/virtual receptionist, stronger contact or booking assistance, local/technical SEO foundations, analytics, performance, and conversion UX materially support the prospect's workflow.
- Consider **Growth + Visibility** when the prospect is ready for deeper local discovery, GEO foundations, advanced measurement, content/social guidance, or agreed automations/integrations.

Do not hard-code a recommendation. Record why the selected package fits this prospect and why the other options are less suitable now.
