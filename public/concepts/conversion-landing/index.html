All answers describe this fictional product concept rather than a live SaaS service.

Is NexaFlow a real product?+

No. NexaFlow is an independent portfolio redesign concept created to demonstrate product positioning, interface design and front-end implementation.

Are the integrations functional?+

No affiliations or live integrations are implied. The interface shows how integration architecture could be presented.

Are the prices or metrics real?+

No. Pricing cards are structural demonstrations, while visible interface values only help explain the UI.

What does this concept demonstrate?+

A conversion-focused narrative, responsive product UI, motion, interactive components and a clear path from problem to action.
