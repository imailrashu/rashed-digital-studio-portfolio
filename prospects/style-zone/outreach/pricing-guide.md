## Package Decision

- **Recommended option:** Business Growth
- **Recommended price:** ₹14,999 one-time
- **Recommendation rationale:** This package best matches the demonstrated opportunity: a polished mobile-first website, clearer contact journey, safe virtual salon assistant, local/technical SEO foundations, analytics, performance work, and conversion-focused UX. It keeps deeper ongoing SEO/GEO, content, social, and automation work outside the initial launch until Style Zone's readiness and goals are confirmed.
- **Why Website Essentials is not the starting recommendation:** It covers the core website but omits the chatbot, stronger local/technical foundation, analytics, performance, and conversion work demonstrated as part of the proposed customer journey.
- **Why Growth + Visibility is not the starting recommendation:** Deeper SEO/GEO, advanced tracking, social/content guidance, and automations would be premature before Style Zone confirms its business information, access, priorities, and ongoing operating capacity.
