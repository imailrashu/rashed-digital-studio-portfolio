# Website and Online-Presence Audit — Style Zone

## Audit Scope

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Official website status:** No official owned website found during the documented search pass
- **Public pages inspected:** Magicpin directory result, Chotu directory result, and exact name/address/phone search results
- **Access limitations:** No owner-controlled website or social account was available to audit. Findings concern the publicly discoverable journey, not private business operations.

## Executive Summary

Style Zone appears in multiple third-party directories at a consistent Dhakuria street address, but no owned website or confidently attributable official social profile was found. The current public journey depends on directory information, includes a conflicting postcode, and does not provide a verified service menu, hours, or owned appointment path. A restrained demo can responsibly show how the verified location and phone contact could be presented in one clear, mobile-friendly experience while leaving all unverified business details out.

## Customer Journey

- **Understand the business quickly:** The salon category and Dhakuria location are discoverable, but across third-party listings.
- **Services:** A detailed, reliable service menu was not found and should be confirmed directly.
- **Location:** The street address is corroborated, while the postcode conflicts.
- **Contact:** One public phone number is listed. WhatsApp and booking-tool availability are not verified.
- **Mobile:** Directory pages are not an owned, branded mobile journey.
- **Trust:** No reviews or ratings are reused in the demo.

## Technical and Accessibility Observations

There is no verified official site to test for its own responsiveness, navigation, accessibility, metadata, or performance. The opportunity is therefore an owned-site gap rather than a claim that an existing website is technically poor.

## Opportunity Statement

Create a private editorial salon concept that gives Style Zone one coherent identity, presents its verified Dhakuria location, and makes the publicly listed phone and directions easy to use. Before any real launch, the salon would need to confirm the phone, postcode, services, hours, imagery, booking policy, and brand assets.
